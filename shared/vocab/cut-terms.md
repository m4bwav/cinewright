# Cut terms

## Vocabulary

| Term | What it is | Default use |
|---|---|---|
| straight cut | picture and sound change on the same frame | the default between shots of one moment |
| J cut | the next shot's sound starts before its picture | lead into a speaker or a new place by ear |
| L cut | the current shot's sound runs on under the next picture | hold a line over the listener's reaction |
| cut on action | the cut falls during a movement that continues in the next shot | hides small mismatches; the eye follows the motion |
| match cut | shape, motion, color or sound repeats across the cut | joins two times or places with a visual rhyme |
| smash cut | an abrupt cut to a sharply different shot or sound level | shock or comedy; use once per scene at most |
| jump cut | a cut inside one shot, same framing, time skipped | a style, never an accident; less than a 30-degree or one-size change reads as one |
| cross-cut | alternate between two lines of action in different places | parallel action, chases, a battle's two fronts |
| cutaway | a shot of something else in the scene, then back | covers a bad frame or a time skip in the main shot |
| insert | a close detail of the main action (a hand, a prop) | shows the object the story turns on |
| reaction shot | the listener or the hit person | sells the line or the blow; often better than the action |
| dissolve | one shot fades through the next | time passing; avoid between shots of one moment |
| fade | to or from black (or white) | starts and ends; one per act at most |
| room tone | the recorded silence of a place | laid under every cut so the gap never drops to digital silence |
| clearing the frame | the subject exits, the empty frame holds 1-2 s, then cut; the next shot may open empty before they enter | joins places or opposite sides of the axis, and hides identity drift at a seam |
| foreground wipe | a large foreground object (a passer-by, a pillar, a truck) covers the whole lens and the cut falls on the covered frames | the strongest join between two generations of one move; plan it in both cards' action |
| soft cut | a dissolve of 1-10 frames | smooths a jumpy action cut without slowing it; fast action seams only |

## Rules

- J and L cuts are sound edits inside a picture cut: picture decides where, sound decides when the ear moves.
- A cut needs a reason (new information, a reaction, a rhythm beat); without one, hold the shot.
- Fades separate scenes; cuts and dissolves connect them. A dissolve needs a reason (time, place, a state of mind) and never patches a scene that will not cut.
- At a generation seam, prefer a join that hides it: cut on a look or a reaction, clear the frame, or wipe on a foreground object (entry cut-points).
