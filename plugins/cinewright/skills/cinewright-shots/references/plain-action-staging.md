# Plain action over stated meaning

## Rules

- No theme-stating staging: a prop or pose placed to spell out what the film means reads as a device. Let the meaning come from what people do.
- Under pressure, give the character one small odd practical action (straightening a cup, picking at a thread, rebuttoning a coat) rather than a big expressive gesture. Write it in the card's action line; the same goes for the script's action lines.
- A look back over the shoulder needs a cause in the scene: a sound, a call, a movement off frame. Name the cause in the card.

## Verify

- No card names a symbol prop; each look back has its cause on the card. A take that breaks this is qc `readable-device`.
