# Group staging

## Rules

- Name each group's shape in the scene bible notes. I: two people facing. A: one person facing a pair, between them. L: one person beside a pair. Every larger group breaks down into I pairs for the camera (after Katz, *Shot by Shot*).
- A speaker addressing a close pair is an I: the pair is one unit and both look the same way.
- Three people spread apart: each exchange is its own two-person axis. Re-establish with a wide when the exchange changes.
- Never cut a three-shot into two two-shots that put the middle person on opposite sides of the frame. Use a two-shot and a single instead (after Mascelli, *The Five C's*).
- A speaker and an audience get opposing looks, like a two-shot.
- Five or more people: pick the two central players. Their line is the scene's axis.
- Keep anything between two people (a lamp, a bottle) out of their opposing close-ups, or right beside one of them. A model draws it on both sides.
- Re-establish a round table often: more than four to six close cards in a row lose the seating.

## Verify

- `continuity diff` MIDDLE-SWAP (warning): the person at `center` in the scene's block appears frame-left in one group card and frame-right in the next.
