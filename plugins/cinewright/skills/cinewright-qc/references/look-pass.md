# Look pass

## Rules

- Run it after the per-element pass (entry review-passes). Each question is one rubric verdict; a fail takes the code in brackets.
- Is the face lit from the upstage side, with a shadow side, not flat from beside the camera? (`flat-light`, check light)
- Does the light on the face come from the side and in the colour of the lamp, fire or window in frame? (`source-mismatch`, light)
- Are rain, haze, smoke or dust visible and lit from behind? (`atmos-unlit`, light)
- Is the subject the brightest or clearest area, unless the card says `tone: hide`? The eye goes to the brightest area before the face. (`tone-hides-subject`, light)
- Does the depth read as the scene's `space`: lines into the distance and staged depth for deep, frontal planes and movement across for flat? (`space-break`, space; the rubric asks it when the scene sets `space`)
- Does the dominant line match the card's `line`? Squint at a small, blurred copy: lines exist only where tones meet.
- Does a moving camera ease in and settle before the last frame? (`move-no-settle`, camera)
- Does a pan or track take at least about 3 s for a thing to cross the frame, with no stepping background? (`pan-strobe`, camera)
- At each cut, is the eye where the cards' `attention` cells plan it? (`attention-jump`, attention; the rubric asks it when the card sets `attention`)
- Across the cut film: `CINE_POST grade measure SHOTS --plan <project>` (cinewright-edit, cinewright-finish) ranks contrast, saturation, warmth, diagonal edges and motion against the plan.
- For a still's light logic (two value families, shadow colour, rims), artwright's craft rows say the same in more detail; use them when artwright is installed.

## Numbers

- Pan speed: at 24-25 fps a thing should take 3-5 s to cross the frame (after Brown, *Cinematography*), about a third of the frame width a second or less.

## Pitfalls

- Judging the moves and the pan speed from numbers: on 354 shots of reviewed local takes, frame difference could not tell a settled camera from a moving subject, and the fastest pans were kept tracking shots. Watch them.

## Verify

- Every look-pass fail in the rubric has its code, a time and the one fix (entry review-passes).
