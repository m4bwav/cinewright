# Performance verbs

## Vocabulary

Playable verbs, each with an object ("...her", "...at him"): accuse, beg, plead with, mock, tease, flirt with, warn, threaten, punish, belittle, gloat at, coax, soothe, comfort, interrogate, confront, dismiss, provoke, challenge, charm, apologise to, reassure, scold, blame, bully, test, needle, win over.

| Result word | Verb to write instead |
|---|---|
| angry | accuse, punish, confront |
| defensive | deflect onto, complain to, warn |
| desperate, weak | beg, plead with |
| smug | gloat at |
| sarcastic | mock, needle |
| sad, self-pitying | complain to, seek comfort from |
| scared | appease, placate |
| sexy | flirt with, tease |
| friendly | win over, make welcome |
| hostile, cold | make unwelcome, dismiss |
| vicious | belittle |
| nervous | hide something from, test |
| calm | soothe, reassure |

Weak verbs to avoid: become, feel, seem, react, ignore, defend.

Physical tasks: pours tea, keeps folding laundry, wipes the counter, searches a drawer, works a stuck lid or door, laces a boot, buttons a coat, counts coins, turns a ring.

Posture and body: head low, shoulders braced; steps back; turns away mid-sentence; sits down heavily; stops what her hands are doing; does not look at him; holds his look.

Face words a model may still need, after the verb and task, never alone (untested): jaw clenched, a small smile, tears in her eyes, brow furrowed, eyes wide, subtle expression, restrained.

## Pitfalls

- A result word alone in a performance clause is a warning, not an error: the face clause above is allowed after a verb.
- Keep mood words (tense, melancholy) for light and colour, out of the performance clause.
