# cinewright-genvideo references

Generated by `cine.py kb index`; do not hand-edit. Open one entry with `python scripts/cine.py kb show <slug> --section <name>` (sections: Rules, Numbers, Vocabulary, Pitfalls, Verify, Notes).

- `camera-angles`: Vertical angle tokens (eye-level to dutch) with what each reads as and where its horizon sits, camera height, dutch pairs, the azimuth number for the 30-degree check, and framing tokens.
- `camera-moves`: The camera-move tokens a shot card allows (one per card), what each does physically, and the prompt words each compiles to.
- `compile-rule`: How a shot card and the bibles become one model's prompt: what goes in, in what order, what is pasted verbatim, and how durations and limits are handled.
- `crowd-prompting`: Prompt crowds in tiers (one hero, a few featured extras, the rest as groups on two or three lanes), bind each reference alone, ban clones, and keep within each model's subject limit.
- `failures-framing`: Failure codes for rendered takes whose composition breaks: edge and background tangents, a competing focal point, horizon faults, floating feet, missing depth, a wrong OTS.
- `failures-motion`: Failure codes for rendered takes (action, opening, end state, anatomy, flicker, physics, text, dialogue, lip lag, sound, seams, spec, loudness): symptom, cause, cheapest fix, rung, check.
- `failures-picture`: Failure codes for rendered takes (identity, wardrobe, props, direction, eyeline, light, look, camera, space, attention): symptom, cause, cheapest fix, rung, check.
- `gemini-omni`: Google gemini-omni-1.2-flash, the Gemini API's default video model since 2026-11: 3-15 s, 360p-4k, 16:9 or 9:16, 24 fps, audio, refs described in words, no seed; 1.1 reroutes to it 2026-12-03.
- `h3-long-takes`: MiniMax H3 past one clip: one-take shots without cuts, long video-to-video in windows, prompts H3 misreads, a refine pass that drops the cloned voice. Windows, latent chain measured.
- `h3-prompt-lint`: What cine.py compile's H3 lint warns about before GPU time (short clips, late or short cuts, fast speech, quoted lines, no soundscape, filler). IV Studio thresholds, unmeasured.
- `kling-3`: Kuaishou Kling 3.0 (kling-v3): 3-15 s, 720p-4k, 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1, native audio, multi-shot up to 6, 3 elements, dialogue as Name (tone): line.
- `ltx-2`: Lightricks LTX-2.5 open weights (local): joint audio, frames 8n+1, sizes multiple of 32, 24 fps, keyframes, one flowing paragraph, quoted dialogue, hard cuts named in sequence.
- `luma-ray-3-2`: Luma ray-3.2: 5 or 10 s, 360p-1080p, six aspects, HDR and EXR, start and end frames, loop, no audio, about 100 words, present-tense mid-action verbs.
- `minimax-h3`: MiniMax H3, local open weights: 4-15 s, 24 fps, joint audio, labelled fields, [Shot N] cuts, camera as type + amplitude + speed, no negative prompt.
- `performance-verbs`: Playable verbs aimed at a person, result words with the verb to use instead, physical tasks, and the face words a video model may still need; for card perform fields and voice intent.
- `recast`: H3 Recast prompt rules for swapping 1-3 people in an existing clip in one pass (one line per person, colour bleed, clip vs reference). Untested; Viggle swap is tested.
- `runway-gen-4-5`: Runway gen4.5: 2-10 s, 720p, 1280:720 or 720:1280 (more ratios from an image), no audio, no refs, seed, 1,000-character prompt, $0.12/s; Aleph edits video.
- `screen-direction`: The fixed phrases for travel, eyeline and frame position that every card and compiled prompt uses, so direction never drifts between shots.
- `seedance-2-5`: ByteDance Seedance 2.5 on BytePlus ModelArk: 4-30 s, 480p-1080p, six aspects, 24 fps, audio, up to 50 refs named @Image 1, Shot 1: lines, no seed or negative field.
- `shot-sizes`: The eight shot-size tokens cards use (EWS to ECU), what each frames, the order used for size steps, and the prompt words each compiles to.
- `veo-3-1`: Google Veo 3.1 on Vertex: 4/6/8 s, 720p-4k, 16:9 or 9:16, 24 fps, audio, 3 asset refs (8 s), timestamp blocks, $0.40/s; Gemini API previews listed ended 2026-10-22, Vertex to 2027-05-04 or later.
- `viggle-remake`: Viggle Remake on local MiniMax H3: video-to-video re-shoot from a six-section prompt and painted frames, keeping motion, camera, cuts and sound; swap people or edit objects. Tested on 16 GB.
- `wan-2-2`: Alibaba Wan 2.2 A14B open weights (local, Apache 2.0): 480p or 720p at fixed sizes, 16 fps, frames 4n+1 (81 = 5 s), silent, style first, Chinese default negative prompt.
