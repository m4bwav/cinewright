# cinewright-analyze learnings

## L-20261111-1 `room-by-eye-too-wide` (2026-11-11)

A Project Nexus level sized its first room as a multiple of a sample box read off contact-sheet thumbnails; it came out about 16 H wide against a film room of about 6 H. Measure in character heights on a full-size frame (room-scale).

## L-20261111-2 `phase-pan-fails-on-cel` (2026-11-11)

Whole-frame phase correlation gave steps of -176 to +200 px between frames on the benchmark's flat cel pan: moving characters swamp a flat background. On cel animation read pans off shared landmarks.
