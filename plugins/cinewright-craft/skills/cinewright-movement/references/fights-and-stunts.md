# Fights and stunts

## Rules

- A fight is a list of exchanges: attack, defense, reaction. One exchange per card; a three-exchange fight is at least three cards plus a wide shot that shows where everyone is.
- Fighters keep their sides of the axis for the whole fight: the attacker frame-left stays frame-left until a move across the line is shown on screen.
- Sell the hit with the reaction (entry weight-and-contact): cut from the swing to the head snapping back. The impact itself can fall on the cut.
- Hide contact with the angle: shoot along the line of a punch so the gap does not show, or from behind the receiver.
- Weapons are props: give each one a description in `bibles/props.json`, the hand it is in, and keep it there across cards.
- Stunts (falls, jumps, crashes): show the take-off in one card and the landing in the next; never the whole arc in one generation.
- Keep the camera simple in fights: static or one slow move. Shaky handheld plus fast action gives melted bodies.
- Many fighters: two in the foreground fighting, the rest as background mass (entry hard-subjects).
- Violence and gore rules differ by model and platform; check the model card and the brief's rating before writing blood or wounds.

## Numbers

- Exchange 1-2 s; fight card 3-5 s; a wide re-establishing shot at least every four fight cards.

## Vocabulary

- exchange, beat (of a fight), sell, cheat, line of the punch, reaction, take-off, landing, wire, pad.

## Pitfalls

- "An intense fight breaks out" gives limbs that merge and weapons that appear and vanish; name each exchange.
- Two fighters swapping sides between cards with no move shown reads as a jump in geography (`continuity diff` POSITION).
