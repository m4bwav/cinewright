# Action coverage

## Rules

- A blow split over two cards on one lens line, medium for the wind-up and close for the finish, reads harder than one card. A crash splits in three: approach wide, impact, reverse breakthrough (Arijon ch. 24).
- Stack action in depth in one still wide: a foreground fighter strikes, a background one falls, a third enters behind. One card, several planes, each with its own beat (ch. 24).
- People or horses crossing between camera and fight give glimpses and raise interest. A low angle helps (ch. 24).
- Chases alternate still long shots with moving closer shots (ch. 24).
- Battles alternate mass wides with one-on-one fights, expanding and contracting. Give each one-on-one a small story (ch. 24).
- No talk in action beyond short commands. Before a short final exchange, a pause: the fighters part, breathe, measure. Build slow, resolve fast, stop soon after (ch. 24).
- Every escape is earned by the character's skill or wit, never luck (ch. 24).

## Numbers

- Handheld moving cards in an action scene: under about 5 s each.

## Verify

- Manual: each split blow uses two cards on one lens line; every escape names the skill that earns it.
