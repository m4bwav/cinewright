# Movements to 1967

## Vocabulary

- **soviet-montage** 1924-30 · look: B&W, high contrast, faces as types, masses · lens: wide and close, low angles · light: hard daylight · move: mostly static · cut: fast collision cuts, graphic contrast, repeated action · sound: silent, score · frame 1.33:1 · ref: Battleship Potemkin (1925), Odessa steps ~00:48
- **german-expressionism** 1919-27 · look: painted slanted sets, stark B&W, heavy makeup · lens: normal · light: hard, cast shadows as characters · move: static · cut: slow, tinted · frame 1.33:1 · ref: Nosferatu (1922), shadow on the stairs ~01:20
- **classical-hollywood** 1930-60 · look: glossy studio, invisible style, glamour close-ups · lens: 35-50mm · light: three-point, soft key, backlight halo, high-key · move: smooth dolly, crane · cut: continuity, shot and reverse · sound: lush orchestral · frame 1.37:1 · ref: Casablanca (1942), airport farewell ~01:35
- **film-noir** 1941-58 · look: B&W, night city, wet streets, blind-slat shadows · lens: wide, deep focus, low angles · light: low-key, hard, 8:1, single source, silhouettes · move: dolly, long take · cut: flashback · sound: voice-over, jazz · frame 1.37:1 · ref: Touch of Evil (1958), opening crane shot 00:00-03:20
- **neorealism** 1945-52 · look: real streets, ruins, non-actors, grey daylight · lens: 28-50mm · light: available daylight · move: static, tracking alongside · cut: plain, long scenes · sound: post-synced · frame 1.37:1 · ref: Bicycle Thieves (1948), final scene ~01:20
- **french-new-wave** 1959-67 · look: Paris streets, natural light, young faces · lens: wide, early zooms · light: available, bounced · move: handheld, wheelchair tracking, long takes · cut: jump cuts, direct address · sound: jazz, location · frame 1.37:1 or 2.35:1 · ref: Breathless (1960), opening car ride 00:00-10:00
