# cinewright-history references

Generated by `cine.py kb index`; do not hand-edit. Open one entry with `python scripts/cine.py kb show <slug> --section <name>` (sections: Rules, Numbers, Vocabulary, Pitfalls, Verify, Notes).

- `applying-styles`: Turn 'shoot it like 1970s New Hollywood, 2.39' into style-bible fields (look, lighting, lens_family, frame_aspect, allowed_moves, history) that compile and diff; no names in prompts.
- `aspect-ratios`: Cinema and delivery aspect ratios with their era, the style-bible frame_aspect string, and how much of a 16:9 render each keeps after the crop.
- `cinematographers`: Signature-device cards for eight directors of photography (Deakins, Lubezki, Storaro, Willis, Doyle, Nykvist, Muller, Kaminski): light, lens and move tags with one reference scene.
- `directors-auteurs`: Signature-device cards for Kubrick, Tarkovsky, Coppola, Scorsese, Spielberg, Lynch, Malick and Varda, each with one reference scene.
- `directors-classic`: Signature-device cards for Hitchcock, Welles, Ozu, Kurosawa, Bergman, Fellini, Leone and Godard, each with one reference scene.
- `directors-modern`: Signature-device cards for Wong Kar-wai, Fincher, Wes Anderson, Tarantino, Nolan, Villeneuve, Bong Joon-ho and Park Chan-wook, each with one reference scene.
- `directors-world`: Signature-device cards for Akerman, Kieslowski, Hou Hsiao-hsien, Claire Denis, Spike Lee, Kathryn Bigelow, Sofia Coppola and Barry Jenkins, each with one reference scene.
- `eras-by-format`: Style cards for eight film-format eras, from Academy B&W nitrate and three-strip Technicolor to CinemaScope, 1970s zoom and grain, 1980s smoke, 1990s bleach bypass, DI teal-orange and large format.
- `genres-action`: Style cards for the western, war, horror, thriller and science fiction; tags for look, lens, light, move, cut, sound, frame and one reference.
- `genres-light`: Style cards for romantic comedy, the musical, documentary, music video and commercial; tags for look, lens, light, move, cut, sound, frame and one reference.
- `lens-terms`: Focal-length bands on full-frame 35 mm with what each does to faces and space, plus the lens words a style card or style bible uses (spherical, anamorphic, zoom, vintage, bokeh, flare).
- `lighting-terms`: Light names, key-to-fill ratios and their words, classic portrait setups, and color temperatures in kelvin, as used in style cards, the style bible's lighting string and shot cards.
- `movements-early`: Style cards for Soviet montage, German Expressionism, Classical Hollywood, film noir, Italian Neorealism and the French New Wave; tags for look, lens, light, move, cut, sound, frame and one reference.
- `movements-late`: Style cards for New Hollywood, Dogme 95, Hong Kong action, J-horror, slow cinema and the modern streaming look; tags for look, lens, light, move, cut, sound, frame and one reference.
