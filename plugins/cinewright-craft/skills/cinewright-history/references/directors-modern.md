# Directors, modern

## Vocabulary

- **wong-kar-wai** · smeared step-printed motion, saturated neon and red, cramped corridors, foreground obstruction, slow motion on a repeated song · ref: In the Mood for Love (2000), noodle-stall stairs ~00:15
- **fincher** · locked-off precise frames, camera moves only with the subject, green-yellow desaturation, low-key · ref: The Social Network (2010), opening bar 00:00-05:00
- **wes-anderson** · center framing, symmetry, flat frontal staging, pastel palette, whip pans, lateral tracking, aspect changes by period · ref: The Grand Budapest Hotel (2014), opening 00:00-05:00
- **tarantino** · low shot from inside a car trunk, long dialogue scenes, chapter titles, crash zooms, film grain · ref: Inglourious Basterds (2009), farmhouse opening 00:00-20:00
- **nolan** · large-format IMAX, practical effects, cross-cut timelines, ticking score, cool steel color · ref: The Dark Knight (2008), bank heist 00:00-06:00
- **villeneuve** · tiny figure in vast space, haze, one-color palettes, brutalist buildings, slow pace, low drone · ref: Blade Runner 2049 (2017), orange haze city ~01:40
- **bong-joon-ho** · genre shifts mid-scene, class shown by levels and stairs, groups staged in depth on 2.39 · ref: Memories of Murder (2003), opening field 00:00-03:00
- **park-chan-wook** · ornate symmetrical design, one-take lateral corridor fight, operatic violence, strings · ref: Oldboy (2003), corridor fight ~00:58
