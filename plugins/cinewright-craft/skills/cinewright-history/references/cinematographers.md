# Cinematographers

## Vocabulary

- **dp-deakins** · one motivated source, silhouettes, soft top light, practical lamps, clean minimal frames · ref: 1917 (2019), opening 00:00-05:00
- **dp-lubezki** · natural light only, very wide lenses close to faces, long continuous takes, magic hour · ref: Children of Men (2006), car ambush ~00:18
- **dp-storaro** · color as meaning, orange against blue gels, 2.00:1 frame · ref: Apocalypse Now (1979), opening jungle fire 00:00-05:00
- **dp-gordon-willis** · top light, eyes in shadow, dark amber interiors, B&W scope · ref: Manhattan (1979), opening skyline 00:00-03:00
- **dp-christopher-doyle** · handheld, step-printing, neon, saturated, wide lens close · ref: Chungking Express (1994), opening chase 00:00-04:00
- **dp-sven-nykvist** · soft window light, simplicity, faces, few lamps · ref: Cries and Whispers (1972), opening 00:00-05:00
- **dp-robby-muller** · available light, saturated primaries at dusk, road and motel · ref: Paris, Texas (1984), opening desert 00:00-05:00
- **dp-janusz-kaminski** · blown-out windows, desaturated, narrow-shutter action, backlit haze · ref: Schindler's List (1993), girl in the red coat ~01:00
