# Directors, auteurs 1960-2000

## Vocabulary

- **kubrick** · one-point perspective, symmetrical corridors, wide lens close, Steadicam following, head-down stare, classical music · ref: The Shining (1980), tricycle corridors ~00:40
- **tarkovsky** · long takes, slow tracking, water and rain indoors, wind in grass, fire, sepia to color · ref: Stalker (1979), railcar into the Zone ~00:37
- **coppola** · top light hiding the eyes, amber tungsten, dark interiors, cross-cut ritual and violence · ref: The Godfather (1972), opening office 00:00-06:00
- **scorsese** · long Steadicam takes, freeze frames, voice-over, pop songs, whip pans, slow motion · ref: Goodfellas (1990), Copacabana entrance ~00:31
- **spielberg** · faces lit in awe looking off screen, long takes hidden in blocking, backlit haze, push-ins · ref: Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981), temple opening 00:00-12:00
- **lynch** · dread in the ordinary, slow push into darkness, red curtains, industrial drone, deep blacks · ref: Blue Velvet (1986), opening 00:00-04:00
- **malick** · magic hour, natural light only, low wide angles, drifting Steadicam, whispered voice-over, nature cutaways · ref: Days of Heaven (1978), harvest at dusk ~00:20
- **varda** · real streets, fiction blended with documentary, time running in real time, mirrors · ref: Cleo from 5 to 7 (1962), opening tarot 00:00-05:00
