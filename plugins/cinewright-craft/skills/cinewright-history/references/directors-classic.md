# Directors, classic

## Vocabulary

- **hitchcock** · point-of-view pairs (a look, then what is seen), dolly zoom, precise storyboarded dolly, the audience knows more · light: studio high-key with key shadows · ref: Rear Window (1954), opening pan 00:00-03:00
- **welles** · deep focus, low angles showing ceilings, 18-25mm close · light: low-key hard pools · move: crane, long take · cut: overlapping sound · ref: Citizen Kane (1941), boarding-house scene ~00:21
- **ozu** · static camera at seated height, 50mm only, frontal symmetrical frames, still-life shots between scenes, cuts across the axis (set crosses_axis with a reason) · ref: Tokyo Story (1953), opening 00:00-05:00
- **kurosawa** · long lens on action, several cameras, weather (rain, wind, fog), wipes, axial cuts · ref: Seven Samurai (1954), battle in the rain ~03:10
- **bergman** · faces in close-up, two faces in one frame, white rooms, silence, soft window light · ref: Persona (1966), opening montage 00:00-06:00
- **fellini** · processions, carnival, wide designed sets, camera weaving through people, circus score · ref: 8 1/2 (1963), opening traffic dream 00:00-04:00
- **leone** · extreme close-up of eyes against extreme wide, long silent standoffs, 2.35:1, sung and whistled score · ref: The Good, the Bad and the Ugly (1966), final standoff ~02:45
- **godard** · jump cuts, direct address, primary red and blue, text on screen, long lateral tracking, scope · ref: Contempt (1963), opening tracking shot 00:00-03:00
