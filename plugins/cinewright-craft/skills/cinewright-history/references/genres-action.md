# Genres, action and dread

## Vocabulary

- **genre-western** · look: big sky, dust, sun-bleached earth, low horizon · lens: wide for land, long for faces, eye close-ups · light: hard sun, hat brims shading eyes · move: slow pan, static · cut: standoff, cross-cut close-ups · sound: wind, spurs, sparse score · frame 2.39:1 · ref: Once Upon a Time in the West (1968), station opening 00:00-12:00
- **genre-war** · look: desaturated, mud, smoke, debris · lens: wide in combat, long for scale · light: hard overcast, fire · move: handheld, narrow shutter · cut: chaotic, point of view · sound: muffled blasts, passing rounds · frame 1.85:1 or 2.39:1 · ref: Dunkirk (2017), opening street 00:00-05:00
- **genre-horror** · look: dark rooms, negative space · lens: wide, deep focus so the background can hide things · light: low-key, single source, underlight · move: slow push-in, point of view · cut: long holds, then a sudden cut · sound: silence, drone, stingers · frame 1.85:1 or 2.39:1 · ref: Halloween (1978), opening point-of-view take 00:00-04:00
- **genre-thriller** · look: cool, clean, controlled · lens: long-lens surveillance, 35-50mm · light: low-key, hard top light · move: slow dolly, locked off · cut: cross-cutting, ticking clock · sound: pulse score · frame 2.39:1 · ref: Sicario (2015), opening raid 00:00-08:00
- **genre-sci-fi** · look: designed hardware, used or clean future, scale · lens: wide anamorphic · light: practical panels, cool, haze · move: slow glide, crane · cut: measured · sound: hum, designed tones · frame 2.39:1 · ref: Alien (1979), the ship wakes 00:00-06:00
