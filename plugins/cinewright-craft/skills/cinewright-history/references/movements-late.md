# Movements since 1967

## Vocabulary

- **new-hollywood** 1967-80 · look: location, naturalistic, muted earth tones, heavy grain · lens: zooms 25-250mm, long lens · light: available and practical, low-key · move: slow zoom-in, handheld, pan · cut: loose, overlapping dialogue · frame 1.85:1 or 2.39:1 · ref: The Conversation (1974), opening zoom on the square 00:00-03:00
- **dogme-95** 1995-2005 · look: raw video, real locations, no set dressing · lens: camcorder wide · light: available only, none added · move: handheld only · cut: jump cuts · sound: location only, no score · frame 1.37:1 · ref: The Celebration (1998), dinner speech ~00:20
- **hong-kong-action** 1986-97 · look: neon, smoke, saturated · lens: wide for action, long for slow motion · light: hard, colored · move: fast tracking, crash zoom, slow motion · cut: rapid, hit repeated across cuts · sound: big foley hits · frame 1.85:1 or 2.35:1 · ref: Hard Boiled (1992), teahouse shootout 00:00-12:00
- **j-horror** 1998-2005 · look: desaturated green-grey, everyday rooms, empty space · lens: wide, static frames · light: flat fluorescent, dim · move: static, slow push-in · cut: long holds · sound: low drone, silence · frame 1.85:1 · ref: Ringu (1998), the TV climax ~01:25
- **slow-cinema** 1975-now · look: everyday detail, landscapes, B&W or muted · lens: 35-50mm from a distance · light: available · move: static long takes, slow tracking · cut: very few, shots of minutes · sound: ambient, silence · frame 1.37:1 to 1.85:1 · ref: Satantango (1994), opening cattle 00:00-08:00
- **streaming-look** 2015-now · look: clean digital, low contrast, lifted blacks, teal shadows · lens: large format, 35-75mm, shallow focus · light: soft big sources, low-key · move: gimbal, slow push-in · cut: coverage-heavy · sound: dense score · frame 2.00:1 or 2.39:1 · ref: Extraction (2020), opening ~00:00-05:00

## Notes

- 2026-10-04: `~` timestamps are approximate and unchecked against a copy.
