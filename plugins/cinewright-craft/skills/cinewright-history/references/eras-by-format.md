# Eras by format

## Vocabulary

- **era-nitrate-academy** 1910-52 · look: B&W, silver-rich blacks, nitrate grain, glow · lens: 35-50mm, diffused close-ups · light: hard tungsten, studio three-point · move: static, dolly · frame 1.37:1 · ref: The Third Man (1949), sewer chase ~01:30
- **era-technicolor** 1935-55 · look: saturated primaries, deep reds, painted skies · lens: 35-50mm · light: bright high-key (slow stock) · move: crane, dolly · frame 1.37:1 · ref: The Wizard of Oz (1939), door to Oz ~00:19
- **era-cinemascope** 1953-67 · look: wide vistas, Eastmancolor, staging across the frame · lens: early anamorphic, faces widen in close-up · light: high-key · move: slow pans, few cuts · frame 2.55:1 then 2.35:1 · ref: Bad Day at Black Rock (1955), opening train 00:00-03:00
- **era-1970s-zoom** 1970-79 · look: Kodak 5247 grain, muted warm, flare · lens: 25-250mm zoom, long lens · light: available, practicals · move: slow zoom-in, handheld · frame 1.85:1 or 2.39:1 · ref: Jaws (1975), beach dolly zoom ~00:22
- **era-1980s-diffusion** 1980-89 · look: diffusion filters, smoke, backlit shafts, neon, pastel · lens: anamorphic flares · light: hard backlight through haze, colored gels · move: Steadicam, crane · frame 2.39:1 · ref: Blade Runner (1982), opening cityscape 00:00-04:00
- **era-1990s-bleach-bypass** 1993-2002 · look: desaturated, silver kept, hard contrast, crushed blacks · lens: narrow shutter for action · light: hard overcast · move: handheld · frame 1.85:1 or 2.39:1 · ref: Saving Private Ryan (1998), Omaha beach ~00:05-00:25
- **era-di-teal-orange** 2000-15 · look: digital grade, teal shadows, orange skin, saturated · lens: anamorphic, wide · light: sunset backlight · move: crane, orbit · cut: fast · frame 2.39:1 · ref: Transformers (2007), desert base opening ~00:03
- **era-large-format** 2015-now · look: large sensor, shallow focus at width, soft low-contrast glass · lens: rehoused vintage lenses · light: soft, natural · move: gimbal, slow · frame 2.39:1 · ref: Rogue One (2016), farm prologue ~00:00-07:00

## Notes

- 2026-10-04: Kodak 5247 100T introduced 1974 (Wikipedia film stock list). `~` timestamps are approximate and unchecked against a copy.
