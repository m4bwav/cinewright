# Genres, light and short form

## Vocabulary

- **genre-rom-com** · look: bright, warm, the city in season · lens: 35-50mm two-shots · light: high-key, soft, 2:1 · move: walk-and-talk tracking · cut: shot and reverse, montage · sound: pop songs · frame 1.85:1 · ref: When Harry Met Sally (1989), the deli ~00:42
- **genre-musical** · look: saturated color, designed spaces · lens: wide, full bodies in frame · light: theatrical spots, color shifts on the beat · move: crane, long takes with the dance · cut: on the beat, few cuts in a number · sound: song on playback · frame 2.39:1 · ref: La La Land (2016), freeway opening 00:00-05:00
- **genre-documentary** · look: real places, imperfect frames · lens: zoom, long lens · light: available · move: handheld, follow · cut: interview and observation, jump cuts · sound: sync location, room tone · frame 1.78:1 · ref: Grey Gardens (1975), opening ~00:00-05:00
- **genre-music-video** · look: one bold idea, graphic color · lens: wide, fisheye allowed · light: hard colored, strobes · move: one move per song section · cut: on the beat, 1-2 s shots · sound: the track · frame 1.78:1 or 9:16 · ref: Around the World (1997, Michel Gondry), whole video 00:00-04:00
- **genre-commercial** · look: polished, product as hero, clean · lens: macro for product, 50-85mm faces · light: high-key soft, rim on the product · move: slow push-in, orbit · cut: 15-60 s, product last · sound: voice-over, logo sting · frame 1.78:1 or 9:16 · ref: Apple 1984 (1984, Ridley Scott), whole spot 00:00-01:00
