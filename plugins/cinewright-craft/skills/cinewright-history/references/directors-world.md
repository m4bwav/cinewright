# Directors, world and independent

## Vocabulary

- **akerman** · static frontal long takes, domestic work in real time, low fixed camera height, symmetry · ref: Jeanne Dielman (1975), opening kitchen 00:00-10:00
- **kieslowski** · one dominant color per film, filtered light, extreme close-ups of small objects · ref: Three Colours: Blue (1993), opening drive 00:00-04:00
- **hou-hsiao-hsien** · long static takes from a distance, depth through doorways, lamps, events left off screen · ref: The Assassin (2015), black-and-white prologue 00:00-08:00
- **claire-denis** · bodies and skin, close textured handheld, elliptical cuts, dance · ref: Beau Travail (1999), final dance ~01:27
- **spike-lee** · double dolly (the actor floats), dutch angles (set allow_dutch), saturated heat colors, direct address · ref: Do the Right Thing (1989), opening dance 00:00-04:00
- **bigelow** · several handheld cameras, zooms, tension in waiting, grain · ref: The Hurt Locker (2008), opening bomb 00:00-10:00
- **sofia-coppola** · soft pastel, natural light, loneliness in rooms, long held two-shots, dreamy pop · ref: Lost in Translation (2003), opening arrival 00:00-05:00
- **barry-jenkins** · saturated color, faces looking into the lens, circling camera, strings · ref: Moonlight (2016), opening circling take 00:00-04:00
