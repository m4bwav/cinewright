# Chion's terms

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| diegetic | the sound exists in the story world (a radio, rain, a voice) | the default for effects and ambience |
| non-diegetic | outside the story world (score, narration) | music and voice-over |
| on-screen / off-screen | the source is in frame or in the scene but out of frame | off-screen sound widens the world beyond the render |
| acousmatic | heard without its source being seen | suspense; a battle beyond the hill |
| acousmêtre | a voice whose speaker is never or not yet shown | a power held back until the reveal |
| synchresis | a sound and an image event fused because they coincide | any sound on the exact frame of an impact reads as that impact |
| added value | the sound makes the picture seem to show more than it does | a weak generated hit becomes strong with a strong sound |
| empathetic | sound that follows the scene's feeling | most scoring |
| anempathetic | sound indifferent to the feeling (a tune playing on through a death) | irony, horror |

## Rules

- Decide per scene which sound is on screen, which off, and whether the score is empathetic or anempathetic; write it in `sound/plan.md`.
- Use synchresis to fix generated motion: put the impact sound on the frame of contact, not where the model's audio put it.
- Use off-screen and acousmatic sound for anything too large or too hard to render (cinewright-movement, entry hard-subjects).

## Pitfalls

- Every sound on screen and in sync: the world ends at the frame edge.
