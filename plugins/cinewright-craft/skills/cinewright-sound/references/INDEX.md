# cinewright-sound references

Generated by `cine.py kb index`; do not hand-edit. Open one entry with `python scripts/cine.py kb show <slug> --section <name>` (sections: Rules, Numbers, Vocabulary, Pitfalls, Verify, Notes).

- `battle-sound`: Carry a battle's scale with sound: near, mid and far layers, weapons only in the near layer, perspective that follows shot size, silence at the turn; sound implies the unrendered army.
- `chion-terms`: Name what sound does to the picture with Chion's terms (diegetic, non-diegetic, synchresis, added value, acousmatic sound, empathetic and anempathetic) and choose one on purpose per scene.
- `conform-to-tempo`: Put recorded effects or foley on a music cue's tempo with the lightest edit: tempo match, then per-hit warp, then cut. Rubber Band in ffmpeg, stretches 0.75-1.35, quiet edit points, report unheard.
- `cut-terms`: Names of cuts and transitions shared by picture and sound editors (J and L cuts, match, smash, jump, cross-cut, cutaway, insert, dissolve), with what each does and when it is the default.
- `generated-audio`: Treat a video model's audio as a guide track: resample to 48 kHz, keep usable dialogue, replace or cover its effects and ambience, hide generation seams under room tone.
- `loudness-targets`: Delivery loudness targets checked 2026-10-04 (EBU R128 v4 and s2, ATSC A/85:2026, Netflix, Spotify) with tolerance, true peak, how each is measured, and the matching qc loud preset.
- `mix-and-loudness`: Mix in a fixed order (dialogue, effects, ambience, music) at 48 kHz, then reach the stated target once on the whole mix: gain and limiter, a linear loudnorm pass, qc loud.
- `music-spotting`: Spot music on a locked cut: decide where each cue starts and stops and what it does, start and end cues on story beats rather than cuts, keep dialogue clear, and record every cue's source and rights.
- `sound-layers`: Build every scene from the same six layers (dialogue, foley, hard effects, ambience, room tone, music), keep them on separate tracks, and deliver dialogue, music and effects stems beside the mix.
