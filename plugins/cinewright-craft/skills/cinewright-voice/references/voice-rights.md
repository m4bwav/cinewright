# Voice rights and consent

## Rules

- Designed voices (from a text description) and licensed library voices are fine: write "original designed voice", or the library and licence, in `rights`.
- A real person's voice only with their written consent for this use, named and dated in `rights`. The user's own voice counts.
- No sound-alikes of living actors, singers or public figures, and no real names in design prompts. Hosted services block celebrity clones.
- Union actors: SAG-AFTRA's 2026 terms need separate written consent with a specific description of the use.
- Disclose synthetic voices that sound like real people: the EU AI Act Art. 50 deepfake duty applies from 2026-08-02. A credit saying the voices are AI-generated covers the common case.
- Keep the watermark some engines add to every output (Chatterbox Perth).

## Notes

- 2026-10-07: the US NO FAKES Act passed Senate Judiciary on 2026-06-18 and is not law; the Tennessee ELVIS Act and New York's digital-replica law are in force. Not legal advice: a commercial release with a cloned real voice needs a lawyer.
