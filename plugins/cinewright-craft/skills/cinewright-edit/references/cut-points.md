# Cut points

## Rules

- Cut where one idea ends and the next begins: the moment the viewer has got it. People blink at that moment, so the cut sits with the blink or just ahead of it (after Murch, *In the Blink of an Eye*). The card's `beats` mark the candidate points.
- Prefer the cut on a look (someone looks off, cut to what or whom they see) and the cut to a reaction over the cut on action. Generated motion rarely matches between takes; a look needs no motion match.
- A line of dialogue has two good cut points. Cut away during the line and it is taken at face value. Hold past it until the listener's face shows what they make of it. A cut between the two feels too long or too short.
- Do not cut to each speaker as their line starts. Attention moves to the listener while the speaker is still talking: cut there and let the line run on (an L cut).
- When one person acts on another (accuses, begs), the cut goes to the one acted on, mid-line, then back as they act in return; reply gaps: entry reaction-timing (Rabiger ch. 16).
- Out point test: after the assembly, play each cut at speed and stop on the out point twice. If the two stops land more than 2 frames apart, the cut is not understood yet; rethink it.
- Fine placement (Murch's house notes, one editor's habits, untested on generated clips): during speech, cut on or near a fricative (s, sh, f, v, th); on a walker, cut on the shift of weight, not the footfall; on an exit, cut when the person must leave but has not left; never on a blink itself, almost always just before it.
- After a look and its point-of-view shot, return to the same head position and expression. Three point-of-view shots in a row free you from this.
- Open the incoming shot on the start of a movement, and allow one or two extra frames for the eye to find its point of interest.
- At a generation seam, hide the join (shared vocab cut-terms): a look or reaction, a cleared frame, or a foreground wipe.

## Pitfalls

- Finding the out point by stepping frame by frame: the cut loses the rhythm it would have at speed.
