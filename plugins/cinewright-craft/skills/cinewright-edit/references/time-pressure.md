# Time pressure inside a shot

## Rules

- Rhythm comes from the pressure of time inside each shot, not only from shot lengths. Tag every shot in `edit/cut.md`: `still` (nothing moves, or one small motion), `flowing` (steady motion: water, walking, a slow move) or `rushing` (fast motion, crowded action).
- A `still` shot cut short reads as a slide, not a moment. Flag any `still` shot under about 3 s, and any run of three or more short `still` shots: hold them, merge them, or swap one for a `flowing` take.
- Something should move in a held frame (a curtain, smoke, rain, a flame). A held shot with nothing moving reads frozen in a generated clip.
- Shots that will not sit together after two or three orderings were planned wrong. Regenerate the weak shot from a revised card; do not keep reshuffling the cut.
- Long takes carry over only partly: generated clips run 5-15 s, so one unbroken clip is the long take. Hold it whole rather than cutting into it, and let a contemplative scene run on fewer, longer shots.

## Pitfalls

- Matching pace by shot length alone: four 2 s `still` shots feel slower and choppier than two 4 s `flowing` ones.

## Verify

- Every shot in `edit/cut.md` has a time tag; no `still` shot is under 3 s unless the note says why.
