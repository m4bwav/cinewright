# Lighting terms

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| key / fill / back | main light / light that lifts the shadow side / rim light from behind that separates the subject |
| practical | a lamp, candle or screen seen in the shot that motivates the light |
| motivated | light that seems to come from a source the scene has |
| hard / soft | small or far source, crisp shadows / large or near source, gentle shadows |
| high-key / low-key | bright, low contrast / dark, high contrast, much of the frame in shadow |
| silhouette | subject dark against a bright background |
| negative fill | black surface that deepens the shadow side |

| Key:fill | Words | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1:1-2:1 | even, soft, bright | comedy, commercials |
| 4:1 | strong contrast, shadow side dark | drama |
| 8:1+ | deep shadow, one side of the face lost | noir, horror |

| Setup | Key position | Shadow |
|---|---|---|
| butterfly | high, in front | small shadow under the nose |
| loop | 30-45 degrees off the nose | small nose shadow toward the cheek |
| Rembrandt | 45 degrees, high | triangle of light on the far cheek |
| split | 90 degrees | half the face dark |

| Source | Kelvin | Words |
|---|---|---|
| candle, flame | about 1,850-1,900 | deep orange candlelight |
| tungsten | 3,200 | warm tungsten |
| golden hour sun | about 3,500 (varies) | low golden sun |
| daylight | 5,500-5,600 | neutral daylight |
| overcast, shade | 6,500-7,500 | cool, blue shade |
| blue hour | sky well above 10,000 (varies) | deep blue dusk |

Numbers marked "varies" are working figures, not measurements.
