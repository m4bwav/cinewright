# Nonfiction honesty for AI explainers

## Rules

- Order may change for clarity; cause may not be invented. Putting B after A must not tell the viewer that A caused B when the record does not say so.
- A generated picture is a stand-in, never the event. Narration speaks of the kind of thing ("a ship like this one"), not "this is the ship".
- Label every recreation on screen or in narration the first time it appears.
- Every fact the narration states has two independent sources, kept in `research.md` beside the line. One source: soften it or cut it.
- Guesses about motives or feelings are said as guesses ("he may have feared").
- No generated face stands for a real, named person in a way a viewer could take as a record of them.
- Disclose generated imagery in the credits or description. This rule is ours, not the book's: viewers cannot tell generated pictures from footage, so we say so.

## Pitfalls

- A cut from a generated crowd to a real quote that makes the crowd read as the speakers.
- A date or number kept from one source only because it fit the narration.

## Verify

- Every narrated fact in `script.md` has two sources in `research.md`; every recreation shot has its label.
