# Horror brief and dread

Book craft opinion; the numbers are guides, never gates. Warn only.

## Rules

- `brief.md` names `monster:`, `house:` and `sin:`; warn `MITH-MISSING` by eye when one is absent (ch. 1-4).
- `house:` answers two lines: `no_escape:` (why they cannot leave) and `no_help:` (why no one comes). A mind or a body can be the house (ch. 3).
- `sin:` names who did the simple wrong that let the monster in; the theme-stated beat touches it, and that character gets a payoff card before the end (ch. 4, 12).
- `monster:` gives origin, motive, what woke it now, its limit, and the fear it stands for. Show the limit on screen before the finale uses it. Keep the full explanation in `monster_truth:`; no line of dialogue states it outright, so prompts stay consistent while the film stays vague (ch. 2, 12).
- The opening scare is not the catalyst: the catalyst card has the hero in it (`CATALYST-NOT-HERO`). A cold-open scare on someone else may replace the ordinary-world opening (ch. 12).
- Each act-two stretch names a survival subgoal (a weapon, help, a place, a rescue, the monster's rule) in the scene's `ends_when:`. The space shrinks by act (grounds, house, room, closet); list each act's location (ch. 12).
- Weak midpoint: pick one of a clock starts, a win that wakes the monster, a stranger arrives, a new way to survive, a twist, the monster revealed (ch. 12).
- Slow burn: at least one card per act-two stretch where the monster acts with the hero absent, so the viewer knows more (ch. 11; about one per 20% of runtime on a short).
- Scares: set `dread` 0-10 on cards. Two or more warning cards (isolation, a forbidden place, gear dropped, a foolish move, something only the viewer sees) climb before each scare (8 or more), then dread resets and is rebuilt (ch. 13). `continuity diff` warns DREAD on a scare without two rising cards before it, or two scares back to back.
- Prefer suggested, off-screen scares to gore: video models refuse or melt gore.

## Numbers

- Big scares: a feature has four (ch. 13, one producer's rule). Ours: 60 s one at the end; 2-5 min two or three, on the midpoint and the finale.
