# cinewright-script references

Generated by `cine.py kb index`; do not hand-edit. Open one entry with `python scripts/cine.py kb show <slug> --section <name>` (sections: Rules, Numbers, Vocabulary, Pitfalls, Verify, Notes).

- `dialogue-for-generated-voices`: How many words fit a shot, one speaker per shot, off-screen lines, names a voice model mispronounces, voice strings in the bible, and the dialogue checks in the continuity diff.
- `logline-and-beats`: The one-sentence logline form, act proportions, how many beats a film of a given length holds, and the beat sentence each shot card carries.
- `narration-writing`: Write voice-over against the cut shot list, not before it: each block adds what the picture cannot show, fits the gap before the next sync moment, and reads cleanly aloud.
- `nonfiction-honesty`: For any film that claims to be true and uses generated pictures: reorder without false cause, stand-in images, labelled recreations, two sources per narrated fact, marked speculation, disclosure.
- `performance-verbs`: Playable verbs aimed at a person, result words with the verb to use instead, physical tasks, and the face words a video model may still need; for card perform fields and voice intent.
- `performance-words`: Write a performance as an objective, a playable verb aimed at a person, a physical task and a posture, never emotion adjectives; the card perform fields and what compile says.
- `scene-turns`: Every scene turns a value from positive to negative or back; how to name the value, the goal and the turning point, and place the turn in one shot.
- `screenplay-format`: Slugline, action, character cue, parenthetical and dialogue layout in plain text, how the slugline maps to the scene bible, and the timing rule of a page.
