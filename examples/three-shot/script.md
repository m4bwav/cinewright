INT. LIGHTHOUSE LAMP ROOM - NIGHT

RAIN hammers the windows. The great brass lens stands dark.

MAREN (60s, silver braid, a thin white scar through her left eyebrow) thumbs open a dented tin matchbox. One match left. She pinches it out.

TOMAS (12, curly black hair, yellow oilskin far too big) watches her from across the lens.

Maren looks at her own hand. It trembles. She stretches her arm out to him.

MAREN
(quietly)
Last one. Your hands are steadier than mine.

Tomas takes the match and closes his fist around it, pressed to his chest. A SHIP'S HORN sounds somewhere out in the dark.
